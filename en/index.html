About

I'm a data & AI product manager and founder of Maré Consultoria, where I help companies and organizations connect data, people, and processes.

My take is simple: information becomes a decision when the data is clean, the team aligned, and the product built for the people who use it. In practice that means data strategy, analytics, and product management – plus facilitation when the conversation is the bottleneck.

I spent nearly 7 years at Greenpeace Brazil, where I was invited by the Executive Director to build the Digital Transformation team (Data & CRM) from scratch and led it for nearly 3 years. I built a team of 5 senior professionals, launched the organization's first Executive Dashboard, and sat on Greenpeace International's Technology Council. Before that, a journalist (UNESP) with stints at Tecnoblog and OneDayTesting. I'm a certified tidyverse instructor by RStudio/Posit and co-organizer of R-Ladies São Paulo.

My path: journalism (UNESP) → digital campaigns → data & analytics → product & data leadership.

Based in São Paulo, working remotely with teams worldwide (PT/EN). Open to senior opportunities in Data, AI, and Product – and to consulting projects through Maré. If your challenge involves data, product, or hard conversations, let's talk.
